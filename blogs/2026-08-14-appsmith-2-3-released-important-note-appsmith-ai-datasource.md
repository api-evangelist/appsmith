Appsmith 2.3 Released - Important Note: Appsmith AI Datasource EOL staceylevine Fri, 08/14/2026 - 11:31 🗑️ Feature Removal - September 30, 2026 - EOL We will be removing the Appsmith AI data source functionality as of September 30, 2026. After upgrading to version 2.3, you will no longer be able to add new Appsmith AI data source connections or queries. Any that you have currently will still continue to work until September 30th, 2026.
