Appsmith Oauth2 Guide Welcome to the comprehensive guide on leveraging OAuth2 with Appsmith. This guide delves into the intricacies of integrating OAuth2—a robust security protocol widely adopted for authentication and authorization in modern web applications—with various services and platforms using Appsmith. Our journey will cover a range of practical scenarios and integrations like Salesforce, Airtable, Supabase and more.
